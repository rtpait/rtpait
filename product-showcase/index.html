How are the web performance and revenue KPIs on this page sourced?

All metrics are compiled automatically by our Daily Unified Telemetry ETL Pipeline from four deterministic sources: Google Search Console API for indexation and organic CTR, our 3-Bucket Traffic Classifier (IP/ASN datacenter and owner filtered) for Bucket A Verified Human Visits and Bucket B AI Crawler Queries, SubID partner network reconciliation for confirmed revenue and RPV under Principle 1, and automated Day-0 release gate validators.

What does Principle 1 (Ground-Truth Actuals Only) mean for revenue reporting?

Principle 1 prohibits mixing pre-launch projections or synthetic estimates with live operational metrics. Until partner network sub-ID conversions settle as confirmed commissions, live revenue and Revenue Per Visitor (RPV) report strictly at $0.00.

How are human visits separated from bots and AI crawlers?

Our telemetry classifier evaluates user-agent signatures and IP/ASN network ranges to partition traffic into three mutually exclusive buckets: Bucket A (Verified Human Visitors, excluding owner/operator sessions and datacenter ASNs), Bucket B (AI Answer-Engine Crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot), and Bucket C (Automated Bots and scrapers).
